The problem

Pricing cannabis in Canada
is genuinely complicated.

Most LPs are pricing on gut feel or outdated spreadsheets. The regulatory chain from landed cost to consumer MSRP involves five layers of markup, two excise calculation methods, and thirteen different provincial models.

📋
CRA excise is dual-method
Dried cannabis uses the higher of $1.00/g or 10% ad valorem. Extracts and edibles use $0.01/mg THC. Most calculators get this wrong.
🏛️
Every province is different
Gov monopoly, hybrid, private retail — each with its own markup chain, tax rate, and additional duty. Ontario alone has a 39% eComm markup on top of wholesale.
💸
Margins disappear in the chain
A product that looks profitable at landed cost can be a loss after excise, reg fees, provincial duties, and board markup stack up.
📊
Comp pricing is manual work
Pulling competitor prices from OCS.ca, SQDC, and BC Cannabis to benchmark your MSRP takes hours. It shouldn't.
